In this Medical Teacher study, researchers interviewed 10 faculty leaders and 15 frontline clinical teachers across a Toronto Department of Medicine. Clinical workload and relationships with learners shaped how faculty experienced these evaluations, and many felt their departments didn't respond to their concerns, which left them isolated and undervalued. Why it matters: if learner evaluations feed promotion files, pair them with direct observation or peer review, and tell faculty how the data are used.
