Number three. Remediation once, risk again: what 758 medical students teach us. This study in Medical Teacher followed five cohorts who started medical school between 2014 and 2018. About 19 percent needed at least one pre-clinical block remediation, and 81 students remediated two to five blocks. No pattern of exam trends flagged in advance who would struggle, but a prior remediation raised the odds of struggling in a future block. Any remediation was tied to extra Step 1 study time and more first-attempt Step 1 failures, but the number of remediations was not. The synthesis: a struggle is a signal, not a sentence. Pair academic plans with social and well-being support, and most learners go on to thrive.
