Number one. Can ChatGPT prep your CCC packet? It finds the praise, and misses the critique. This study in the Journal of Graduate Medical Education took 277 end-of-rotation assessments for 25 residents. De-identifying them took about an hour, and three large language models then labeled the comments in under seven minutes each. Human reviewers found 476 comments, about 82 percent strengths and about 18 percent areas for improvement. Why it matters: the comments a clinical competency committee most needs are exactly the ones the models missed. Let AI compile, but have a human read every constructive comment.

This 2025 national pilot, reported in Hospital Pediatrics, offered six one-hour sessions on negotiation, the job search, promotion, personal branding, and mentorship.
