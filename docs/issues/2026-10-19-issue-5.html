AITrainees

952,000 clinicians, 271 million AI queries: trainees lead the way

  • Cross-sectional study of NPI-verified users of OpenEvidence (Jan 2024-Jun 2026): monthly active users grew from 355 to 703,265; queries per user rose from 18 to 39.5 a month.
  • Residents and fellows are 14.8% of the physician workforce but sent 27.2% of physician queries (43.2 vs 35.3 queries per user-month for attendings). Early-career clinicians queried pediatrics more often.
  • 52% of queries were for clinical decisions, mostly treatment (33%); only 14% were about diagnosis.
Try this: ask your residents on rounds which AI tool they checked and what it said. Make the habit visible so you can coach it.
Patel VR, Finlayson SG, Liu-Hass M, Ray M, Burk-Rafel J, Zack TI, Jena AB, et al. JAMA. Online Oct 15, 2026. doi:10.1001/jama.2026.21868

A concept-map rubric that may flag struggling students early

  • Harvard team built a 5-domain rubric (comprehensiveness, analytic detail, organization, accuracy, overall quality) for student-made "Mechanism Maps" from 134 first-year students.
  • 4 raters agreed good-to-excellent in most domains (accuracy was weakest). For one vignette, each 1-point higher score roughly doubled the odds of finishing above the bottom exam quartile, even after adjusting for quizzes.
Why it matters: an ungraded reasoning exercise could double as an early signal. The authors call for validation at other schools first.
Fischer K, Furfaro D, Iyer AA, Richards JB, Mitchell J, Sullivan AM, Schwartzstein RM. Acad Med (Innovation Report). Online Oct 14, 2026. doi:10.1093/acamed/wvag324

Faculty burnout: 13% at some schools, 41% at others

  • AAMC StandPoint survey, 28 US medical schools, 15,404 faculty (2021-2024): pooled burnout 32%; commitment 74%-95% and satisfaction 62%-86% across schools.
  • Only 67 of 502 departments (13%) had both low burnout and high commitment. Autonomy, purpose, and belonging helped explain department burnout.
Synthesis: track burnout and engagement together, and go find the bright-spot departments to learn from.
Badgett RG, Balas K, Pipas CF, Courand J, Hartwell JL, Dandar VM. JAMA Netw Open. 2026;9(10):e2639005. Online Oct 14, 2026. doi:10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2026.39005
