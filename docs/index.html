☕ Coffee break with Beth
In my own words. Written by Beth, not AI.

How fun to see colleagues at my institution doing great scholarship on standardized ILP Forms. I took a look at their template - I saw areas of overlap with what we ask in our program, and of course some new ideas. I was intrigued by their first question: Please describe your personal and professional core values.

Golly, that's a big one. It sent me into a bit of a spiral wondering if I can define my own core values. I landed on kindness, family, growth. Not sure how well I live these broad values but maybe I should have an ILP with myself once or twice a year to check in on how I'm doing.

- Beth

Well-beingAssessment

Psychological safety, measured in 5 questions

  • One health system put 9 candidate items in its annual trainee survey (400 of 480 answered) and trimmed them to 5: sharing ideas, giving and receiving feedback, expressing uncertainty, and mistakes as learning.
  • One factor, strong fit (CFI 0.99), omega 0.91, r = 0.67 with an external psych safety measure.
Try this: short enough for an end-of-rotation survey, so you can trend it by site or team. Caveat: single-institution evidence.
Tcheroyan R, Roberts MB, Joshi ART, et al. J Grad Med Educ. 2026;18(4):407-414. PMID 42602734. doi:10.4300/JGME-D-25-00666.1

Remediation through residents' eyes: be transparent, make it personal

  • National qualitative study: 11 pediatric residents who had been through remediation (2024).
  • Themes: weak feedback systems, too little transparency and personalization, and a toll on well-being and careers. The authors updated their PD-based remediation model.

EHR dataAssessment

TRACERs: your EHR may already know how each resident orders insulin

  • 3 IM programs built 2 automated, resident-attributed measures of inpatient diabetes care; EHR extraction matched expert review (96%+ sensitivity, 95%+ specificity).
  • In 615 residents and 6,192 admissions, discouraged bolus-only insulin ran 26.7%-42.6% by program, and residents varied more than programs.
Why it matters: a preview of real-time, practice-based data for CCCs, with real barriers (different EHRs, tricky attribution).
Burk-Rafel J, Sebok-Syer SS, Larson I, et al. Acad Med. Online July 28, 2026. doi:10.1093/acamed/wvag239
