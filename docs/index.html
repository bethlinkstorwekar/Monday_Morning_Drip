1AIAssessment

Can ChatGPT prep your CCC packet? It finds the praise and misses the critique

  • 277 end-of-rotation assessments for 25 residents: de-identifying took ~60 min, and 3 LLMs labeled the comments in under 7 min each.
  • Humans found 476 comments (81.5% strengths, 18.5% areas for improvement). LLM recall was 67.8% for strengths but only 23.9% for areas for improvement; 59.7% of comments were labeled accurately overall.
Why it matters: the comments a CCC most needs are the ones the models missed. Let AI compile, but have a human read every constructive comment.
Cooney CM, Slattery R, Cooney DS, Lifchez SD, Park JE. J Grad Med Educ. Aug 2026. PMID 42602997. doi:10.4300/JGME-D-25-01049.1

2GMESelection

Signals rule the interview list, and gold signals rule most of all

  • ERAS 2023-24 data from 6 specialties (peds not included): a signal raised interview odds 4.7-7.4x with few signals, 8.4-18.5x with many, and up to 55-218x for gold signals in 2-tier systems.
  • Step 2 CK, never failing Step 1, applicant type, and in-state status also mattered, but less. Signals helped out-of-state applicants most.
Try this: peds invites can go out Oct 5. Before you screen, agree as a committee on how much a signal should weigh so it's applied consistently.
Guthrie ST, Griffith B, Hammoud MM, et al. Acad Med. Online Aug 3, 2026. doi:10.1093/acamed/wvag206

3PHMFaculty dev

SHINE: six hours that grew early-career PHM women's mentor networks

  • A 2025 national pilot for trainee and early-career women in PHM: six 1-hour sessions on negotiation, job search, promotion, branding, and mentorship.
  • 25 participants (16 matched pre-post pairs): confidence rose in all 12 career domains (Cohen's d 1.06-2.11). Cross-institutional mentors went from 36% to 67%, peer mentors from 12% to 100%.
Why it matters: a light-touch cohort model is easy to borrow for chiefs or PHM fellows. Caveats: small, self-reported, no control group.
"Evaluation of a National Mentorship Pilot Program for Early-Career Women in PHM." Hosp Pediatr. Online Sept 14, 2026. PMID 42732884. doi:10.1542/hpeds.2026-009354

APPD & GME NEWS
ABP opens a 2-year fellowship path. Starting with fellows who enter in July 2028, programs in 7 subspecialties (adolescent medicine, DBP, PEM, endocrinology, ID, nephrology, rheumatology) can petition for a 2-year clinically focused track. They need EPA-based CBME and must extend training for any fellow not ready at 24 months. Applications open early 2027. ABP › Criteria ›
